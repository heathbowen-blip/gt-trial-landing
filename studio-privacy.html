Studio Privacy Notice

This page describes what the Golden Triangle trial signup page collects and how it is used.

What we collect

When you submit the trial form, we collect your first name, last name, email address, and phone number. When you visit the page, the Meta Pixel may collect page-view and lead-event information as described below.

How we use it

  • To create your Mindbody account so you can book your trial classes.
  • To contact you about your trial.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who processes it

  • Mindbody (via Rota.fit / Zapier) — to create your client account.
  • Stripe — to process your $30 payment. Card details are entered on Stripe's checkout page, not on this page.
  • Meta — the Meta Pixel records PageView and Lead events so we can measure how the signup page performs.

Your choices

To ask about, correct, or delete the details you submitted, contact the studio at 919 Bannock St, Denver, CO 80204, or by phone at 303-285-4236.
