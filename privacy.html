IV. Cookies, Pixels and other Tracking Technologies

The technologies we use for automatic data collection on our website include cookies, pixels, ad tags, and web beacons. We use these technologies to recognize you and your device(s) on, off, and across the website and different services and devices. We use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to gather information about your use of our website and to customize your experience using the website.

Our use of cookies. Cookies are small data files that help us keep track of your usage of the website. We use cookies to personalize your experience on our website and to promote our Services to you. You can set your browser to refuse all or some browser cookies, or to alert you when cookies are being sent. Cookies also allow us to track and analyze user behavior to help improve our website.

The cookies we place on your device may include:

  • Essential Cookies are necessary to provide you with the services available through our website. If you refuse them, it will impact your ability to access some of the website’s functions and features.
  • Functional Cookies help us enhance the performance and functionality of our website, but are not necessary for your use. Still, certain functions or features may become unavailable if you refuse them.
  • Analytics Cookies collect information that is used to help us understand the use of our website, the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns, or to help us customize our website to enhance your experience.
  • Target Cookies record your visit to our website, the pages you have visited and the links you have followed, so we can recognize you as a prior visitor and track your activity on our website and others you visit. These cookies remain on your device for us to use during a next visit to our website. In contrast to these “persistent” cookies, some cookies (“session cookies”) are deleted when your browsing session has ended.
  • Third-party cookies. Some content or applications on the website, including advertisements, are served by third parties. We allow these third parties to use cookies on our website to collect information about your online activities over time and across different websites you visit. This information is used to provide advertising tailored to your interests on websites you visit, also known as interest-based advertising, and to analyze the effectiveness of such advertising.

For more information about cookies, see aboutcookies.org. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you may change your browser settings to accept or decline certain types of cookies if you prefer. If you decline certain cookies, it may impact your use or the functionality of the website. Each browser may have its own way for you to manage cookies: Google Chrome; Mozilla Firefox; Internet Explorer; and Safari.

Pixels and other tracking technologies. Pixels are small transparent images embedded in a website, email or ad. To help us better understand visitors to our Site, pixels placed by service providers may gather limited user data, such as your IP address or event data.

You may seek to block tracking pixels by installing a dedicated browser extension or ad-blocking extension for that purpose or by adjusting your browser privacy settings.

Linked Sites. When you click on links, ad banners, and/or social media icons that take you to third-party websites, you will be subject to the privacy policies and terms of those third-party sites. We are not responsible for those websites or the actions of third parties. You should review the privacy policies and user terms associated with third parties.
